# Daily Programming 🚀

> **Trying to become more consistent!**

This repository is where I will be uploading the programming problems I solve regularly.

I'll be solving questions from different platforms like LeetCode, GeeksForGeeks, and others. The problems can be related to DSA, SQL, programming fundamentals, or anything else that helps me improve my problem-solving skills.

I don't want this to be just a collection of code. I want to keep a record of what I solve, how I approached a problem, and how my problem-solving improves over time.

---

## My Journey

I started learning DSA in my **1st year of college** and started solving problems on LeetCode along with it.

Over time, I solved **300+ LeetCode problems**, but one thing I was never really good at was staying consistent.

As college went on, there were a lot of other things happening too. I started exploring different clubs, events, auditions and other activities.

In my **2nd semester**, I started getting involved with the **CCA Research & Development Cell**, and later continued working with the cell in the **AI/ML domain**.

At the same time, I kept exploring different areas of technology. I spent a good amount of time learning **Web Development**, started with the MERN stack, built projects and eventually completed my MERN learning journey.

Because of all this, my DSA and regular problem-solving practice became on-and-off.

Sometimes I would solve questions regularly, then stop for a while, and later start again.

Now I want to change that.

I want to get back to solving problems regularly and, more importantly, **stay consistent this time**.

That's how this repository started.

---

## Why This Repository Exists

I've already solved a lot of problems before, but most of them were scattered across different platforms and my practice was not consistent.

So I thought of keeping everything I solve from now on in one place.

Whenever I solve a problem, I'll try to add the solution here along with a short explanation of the approach and its complexity.

Some problems will be easy.

Some will take multiple attempts.

Some might teach me a completely new way of thinking.

That's completely fine.

The main idea is to keep learning and keep showing up.

## A New Entry Every Day

The idea is pretty simple:

**Solve → Understand → Document → Commit**

I'm not setting a target like solving 5 or 10 problems every day.

Some days I might solve one problem. Some days I might solve more.

The important part is to keep the habit going.

Even if I only have time for one problem, that's still better than doing nothing.

## Looking Back

I want this repository to eventually become a record of my programming journey.

When I look back at the older solutions, I should be able to see how my thinking and problem-solving approach have changed.

Maybe I'll find solutions that I could write much better today.

Maybe I'll find problems that once took me hours but later become easy.
